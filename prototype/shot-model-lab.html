Shot model lab

The cursor sets your aim and release timing sets your launch speed; every shot is then simulated against a real rim and backboard, with noise from movement, the contest and a universal floor. Defenders’ hands can block it for real.

The game: two sides take turns with the ball against the same defense, first to 21. You have 14 seconds to get a shot off. A miss is a live ball: run to it before a defender does and you keep the possession. A foul on a shot sends you to the line. Untick Game rules under Tuning for free shooting.
Triple threat: every possession, and every rebound you catch, starts with the ball in your hands and your dribble still to come. From there you can go up and shoot, pump fake, jab or pivot with WASD (they move your free foot, not you), or press Shift to put the ball on the floor and go. Once you pick it up again the dribble is gone.
Shooting: press the left mouse button to jump, and let it go to let the ball go. Every shot is the same jump: your legs load for a moment, then you are in the air for about 0.7 s. Come down still holding the ball and it is travelling. You carry half the speed you arrived with into the air, plus up to 5 ft/s the way you hold WASD as you take off, and nothing steers you after that. So hold away from the rim for a fadeaway, toward it for a runner, or against your own momentum to stop. Let go before your feet leave the floor (a quick tap) and it is a pump fake: the ball goes up, you stay down, your dribble is dead (out of a triple threat you keep it), and a defender who bit may be in the air.
The release line: away from the rim, the ball has to be let go on the meter’s line. For most jumps that is the top of the jump: the set shot. Jump while driving at the rim from about 7 to 14 ft and the line is on the way up instead: a floater, off before a shot blocker is there, with more arc and less touch. Moving toward or away from the rim moves the line (earlier going in, later fading), and the meter shows where it is. Moving any other way costs touch, not aim: you never have to aim against your own momentum. To bank a shot, have the cursor on the backboard, clear of the rim, when you jump: the marker turns green where a bank can go in.
Finishes: jump inside 4 ft, on a drive inside about 7 ft, or out of gather steps inside about 12 ft, and it is a finish. A finish has no release line and no aim, wherever you let go: your hand finds the rim, so the cursor is free, and in the air it is the ball. Close to your body, it puts the ball there on a straight arm, to either side of you, behind you, or straight up, and the further out you hold it the lower it is. Out at the rim or beyond, the ball is up in a shooting form. Nothing protects it but where it is: a defender is a body and an arm, and the ball has to get past the arm. Let go on the way up to beat a shot blocker to it, at the top, or on the way down to wait them out, at the cost of control. Let go close: from further out than 4 ft a finish is a heave, and worse for every foot. Get the ball over the rim, near the top of a running jump right at the basket, and it is a dunk. Flying into a defender who was set is a charge; into one who was not, a bump that costs the shot.
Dribbling: the ball bounces toward your cursor. Keep it on the side away from the defender; the farther out the cursor, the longer and more exposed the dribble. Switch sides for a crossover. Slow down with the cursor pulled in tight and the ball hangs up like a pull-up; a defender who respects your shot steps up, and that’s the moment to explode past. Nothing is a button: the defender just reacts to what they saw a moment ago.
Gather: press Shift to pick up your dribble. On the move you get two gather steps: each foot follows WASD until it plants (a ghost circle shows where it will land), so you can steer it mid-step. Changing direction on the second step is a euro; letting go of WASD on the second step is a jump stop. Jump whenever you want to go up. Gathered while stopped, your dribble is dead: the cursor holds the ball and turns your body around the pivot foot, and WASD moves your free foot. The first foot you move is the one on the ball’s side. You can’t dribble again until you shoot.
Close in without driving, from about 4 to 8 ft, a set shot is part finish: aim and timing matter less the closer you are.

Play online: two players

Host a game and send the other player the link. The game starts when they open it. Each of you plays your own side. When you don't have the ball you are the on-ball defender: WASD to move, the cursor is your hand (point it at the ball handler to contest a shot, get it onto the ball to take it), the mouse button to jump. Keep the cursor close to your body to reach straight up, or further away to reach out lower. Reaching into the handler, getting a hand on a shooter’s arms or running into them is a foul. After a miss, the mouse button jumps for the ball for both of you.

If they already have this page open, they can enter the code there instead.

Display

Puts the meter beside your cursor, with its release line level with it, so you can time a shot without looking away from your aim. Off, it stays beside your player.
