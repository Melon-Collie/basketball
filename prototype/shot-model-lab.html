Shot model lab

The cursor sets your aim and release timing sets your launch speed; every shot is then simulated against a real rim and backboard, with noise from movement, the contest and a universal floor. Defenders’ hands can block it for real.

The game: two sides take turns with the ball against the same defense, first to 21. You have 14 seconds to get a shot off. A miss is a live ball: run to it before a defender does and you keep the possession. A foul on a finish sends you to the line. Untick Game rules under Tuning for free shooting.
Jumpers: WASD to move, cursor to aim. Hold the mouse button and let go when the meter reaches the line. Moving shots drift with your momentum, so aim against it. Put the cursor on the backboard beside the rim to bank it: the marker turns green where a bank can go in from your spot. A clean look (nobody within about 8 ft, feet set) with a perfect release goes in about 60% of the time, and its misses rim out instead of clanging. Let go within the first 150 ms to pump fake instead: the ball comes back down, your dribble is dead, and a defender who bit may be in the air. Hold the key that moves you away from the rim as you start a jumper (within the first 150 ms) to commit to a fadeaway: a fixed fade with its own, later release line on the meter. Hold a diagonal (S+A or S+D) for a side fade: a smaller release shift, but the fade pushes the ball sideways, so aim against it.
Finishes: drive at the rim and click inside about 7 ft. The cursor picks which side of the rim you finish on; finish away from the defender to shield the ball. Keep holding to hang in the air and wait out a shot blocker, at the cost of control. Hold Shift as you click to try a dunk.
Dribbling: the ball bounces toward your cursor. Keep it on the side away from the defender; the farther out the cursor, the longer and more exposed the dribble. Switch sides for a crossover. Slow down with the cursor pulled in tight and the ball hangs up like a pull-up; a defender who respects your shot steps up, and that’s the moment to explode past. Nothing is a button: the defender just reacts to what they saw a moment ago.
Gather: press Space to pick up your dribble. On the move you get two gather steps: each foot follows WASD until it plants (a ghost circle shows where it will land), so you can steer it mid-step. Changing direction on the second step is a euro; letting go of WASD on the second step is a jump stop. Click whenever you want to go up. Gathered while stopped, your dribble is dead: the cursor holds the ball and turns your body around the pivot foot, and WASD moves your free foot. The first foot you move is the one on the ball’s side. You can’t dribble again until you shoot.
Floaters: click while driving from about 7 to 14 ft. It’s a lenient timed release whose arc clears a rim protector who stays home. Keep driving instead and you get a layup.

Play online: two players

Host a game and send the other player the link. The game starts when they open it. Each of you plays your own side. When you don't have the ball you are the on-ball defender: WASD to move, the cursor is your hand (point it at the ball handler to contest a shot, get it onto the ball to take it), Space to jump. Keep the cursor close to your body to reach straight up, or further away to reach out lower. Reaching into the handler, getting a hand on a shooter’s arms or running into them is a foul. After a miss, Space jumps for the ball for both of you.

If they already have this page open, they can enter the code there instead.

Connect without the link

The link relies on a free public service to introduce the two browsers. If it is down, you can do its job by hand: one player hosts and sends the other a long code; the other joins with it and sends a code back.

Display

Puts the meter beside your cursor, with its release line level with it, so you can time a shot without looking away from your aim. Off, it stays beside your player.
